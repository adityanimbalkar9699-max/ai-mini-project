01. Forward Chaining (Data-Driven)

Starts with observed evidence (symptoms in Working Memory) and searches antecedent conditions. Evaluates rules via Modus Ponens ($P \rightarrow Q, P \vdash Q$), deriving new conclusions into Working Memory.

Application: Primary differential diagnosis screening.
02. Backward Chaining (Goal-Driven)

Starts with a suspected hypothesis ($Q$) and queries the Knowledge Base recursively. Decomposes goal into subgoals (premises), proving hypothesis or isolating missing clinical evidence.

Application: Emergency rule-outs & hypothesis verification.
Q: Conflict Resolution Strategy?

Ranks rules by Specificity (highest proportion of satisfied premises) and Refractoriness (rules fired once do not refire in the same cycle).

Q: Why Symbolic AI over LLMs?

Medicine requires 100% auditability and zero hallucination risk. Every decision has a deterministic proof trail grounded in clinical guidelines.
